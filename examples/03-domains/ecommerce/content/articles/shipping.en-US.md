# How delivery works

Delivery is free over $50, and $5.99 below that. We pack your order as soon as your payment goes
through, and from then on your order page shows where the parcel has got to, step by step.

You can cancel until the moment the carrier picks it up. If you have already paid, the money goes
straight back to your card.
